Case study - Supply - 2024

Regional GPU supply program

Challenge

Distributed sites needed matched accelerator batches with a single warranty narrative instead of ad hoc marketplace lots.

Approach

Consolidated demand into rolling tranches, sourced through authorised routes, standardised on two board partners for the program duration.

Outcome

Reduced configuration drift; support tickets tied to mismatched batches dropped after the second wave (illustrative metrics).
